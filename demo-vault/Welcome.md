# Welcome to your vault

This is a plain-markdown vault — every note is a `.md` file on disk, just like Obsidian.

## Things that work right now

- **File explorer** on the left with folders and notes
- **Tabs** across the top
- A **CodeMirror** editor with live markdown highlighting
- **Save** with `Cmd+S` (unsaved tabs show a dot)
- Live **file-watching** — edits made outside the app show up automatically

## What's coming

- `[[wikilinks]]` and backlinks
- Graph view
- Command palette (`Cmd+P`)
- The **AI research agent**: conversational capture, RAG over your notes, suggestion documents, and change-tracking
